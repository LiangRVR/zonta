Greetings! We Are Zonta Club of Naples!

Zonta Club of Naples is part of tens of thousands of professionals worldwide who demand equality and the end of violence against women and girls. We seek to eradicate human trafficking, domestic violence, and child marriage so no woman lives in fear.

We say NO to violence and YES to equal opportunity and rights for women and girls of Collier County.
