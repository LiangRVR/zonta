Greetings! We Are Zonta Club of Naples!

Zonta Club of Naples is part of tens of thousands of professionals worldwide who demand equality and the end of violence against women and girls. We seek to eradicate human trafficking, domestic violence, and child marriage so no woman lives in fear.

We say NO to violence and YES to equal opportunity and rights for women and girls of Collier County.

What We Do

Making a meaningful impact through service, advocacy, and education

Community Service

We actively engage in service projects that make a meaningful impact in Naples and beyond, supporting women and girls in our community through direct action and support programs.

Advocacy & Justice

We advocate for gender equality and work to advance the status of women worldwide through local and global initiatives, fighting for equal rights and opportunities.

Direct Action

We take concrete steps to combat human trafficking, domestic violence, and discrimination while supporting law enforcement and providing resources to those in need.
