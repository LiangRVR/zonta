Scholarships

Investing in the Future of Women in Our Community

Young Women in Public Affairs Award

Recognizing young women ages 16-19 who demonstrate leadership skills and commitment to public service and civic causes.

  • Open to high school students
  • Focus on leadership and volunteerism
  • Local and international opportunities
  • Annual awards program

Women in Technology Scholarship

Supporting women pursuing undergraduate degrees in technology-related fields and applied sciences.

  • Undergraduate programs
  • STEM fields focus
  • Merit-based awards
  • Career development support

Jane M. Klausman Women in Business Scholarship

Assisting women pursuing undergraduate or master's degrees in business-related fields.

  • Business degree programs
  • Graduate and undergraduate
  • Career-focused support
  • International recognition
