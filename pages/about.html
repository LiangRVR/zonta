Who We Are

Empowering Women Through Service and Leadership Since 1919

Our Story

The Zonta Club of Naples has been a cornerstone of women's empowerment in Southwest Florida for decades. As part of Zonta International, we are connected to a global network of professionals committed to advancing the status of women worldwide.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to building a better world for women and girls through service, advocacy, and fellowship. Our members are passionate professionals who work together to create positive change in our community and beyond.

Our Values

We believe in equality, justice, and the inherent dignity of every woman and girl. Through our programs, scholarships, and advocacy work, we strive to create opportunities and eliminate barriers that prevent women from reaching their full potential.

Our History

Zonta International was founded in 1919 in Buffalo, New York, and has grown to become a global organization with clubs in over 60 countries. The Zonta Club of Naples continues this proud tradition of service and advocacy in our local community.
