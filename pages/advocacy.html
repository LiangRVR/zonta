Our Service Areas

Making a difference in critical areas affecting women and girls

Human Trafficking Prevention

We work to eradicate human trafficking through education, awareness campaigns, and support for survivors and law enforcement.

Domestic Violence Support

We provide resources and support for survivors of domestic violence while advocating for stronger protections and services.

Education & Scholarships

We invest in the future by providing educational opportunities and scholarships to deserving women in our community.

Community Education

We educate the community about women's rights, gender equality, and violence prevention through workshops and programs.

Economic Empowerment

We support women's economic independence through mentorship, resources, and opportunities for professional development.

Global Partnership

As part of Zonta International, we contribute to global initiatives that improve the lives of women and girls worldwide.

Zonta Club of Naples

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering women through service and advocacy.

Part of Zonta International - a global organization of professionals working to advance the status of women worldwide.
